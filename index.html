About Wizluxe

Wizluxe Furniture & Interior is a trusted furniture and interior design company based in Isuaniocha, Awka North, Anambra State, Nigeria. We specialize in creating premium custom furniture and modern interior solutions for homes, offices, hotels, schools, and commercial spaces. Our mission is to combine quality craftsmanship, elegant designs, and durable materials to create spaces our clients love.

Our Services

Custom Furniture

We design and build custom wardrobes, beds, sofas, TV stands, dining tables, kitchen cabinets, and more.

Interior Design

Professional interior design solutions for homes, offices, hotels, restaurants, and commercial spaces.

Installation & Finishing

Expert installation, finishing, repairs, and maintenance with quality craftsmanship and attention to detail.
